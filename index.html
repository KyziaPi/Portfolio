THESIS PROJECT AUF Medical Center Partnership

RefraScan – AI-Based Fundus Image Screening System

Problem Addressed: Manual refractive error screening requires extensive specialist evaluation. High risk of data leakage when splitting image datasets at image-level rather than patient-level. Hand-written records are hard to make exploratory data analysis out of.

Solution & Features: Developed a clinical decision-support web application for the Lasik Surgery Clinic at AUF Medical Center. Trained a deep learning classifier (ResNet) to categorize fundus images into myopia, hyperopia, and emmetropia while implementing strict patient-level splitting across 1,000+ observations. Digitized the patient records of AUFMC Lasik Surgery Clinic for exploratory data analysis and future research.

Shiftify – Interactive Web-Based Sliding Puzzle Platform
